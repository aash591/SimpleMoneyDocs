A Money Lover alternative that reads your bank SMS

Updated 15 November 2026

Money Lover is a popular expense manager. If you prefer an app with no account, bank SMS reading on your own phone, and built-in loan and debt tracking, try SimpleMoney.

Why people look for a Money Lover alternative

Every expense app makes different trade-offs. People usually switch to get one or more of these: automatic transactions from bank SMS, an app that works with no account, better handling of loans and EMIs, or more control over where their data lives. SimpleMoney is built around those.

What SimpleMoney offers

  • Bank SMS reading on your phone, with no bank login. How it works.
  • No account, works offline. Your records stay on your phone.
  • Loans, EMIs and debts tracked next to your daily spending.
  • Recurring payments and subscriptions with a six-month forecast.
  • Multi-currency accounts with a combined total.
  • Statement import from PDF, CSV and Excel, including Google Pay, PhonePe and Paytm.
  • An optional AI assistant over MCP for Claude and ChatGPT, where you approve every change. Learn more.
  • Encrypted backup to your own Google Drive, and an app lock.

Money Lover: what to know

  • Money Lover offers a free download on its website and syncs across devices.
  • Its premium plans, bank connections and account requirements change over time, so read its current pricing and features first.
  • If privacy matters to you, check where your data is stored and what permissions the app asks for. SimpleMoney keeps records on your phone.

Facts about other apps come from their public pages and can change. Check their current listings before you decide. SimpleMoney is made by the author of this page.

Switching from Money Lover

If your old app can export a CSV or Excel file, you can bring the history in with More → Import statement, then pick which column is the date, amount and description. The app remembers that layout for next time. See the import guide.

Frequently asked questions

Is SimpleMoney a good Money Lover alternative?

If you want automatic SMS tracking, offline use with no account, and loan and debt tracking, yes, it is designed for that. If you need something else, such as iPhone support, another app may suit you better.

Can I move my data from Money Lover?

If Money Lover can export CSV or Excel, you can import that file into SimpleMoney and map the columns.

Is SimpleMoney free?

Yes. Tracking is free. Pro adds unlimited budgets, every look, the AI assistant and removes ads.
