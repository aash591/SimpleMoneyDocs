The best expense manager for Android that works offline

Updated 15 November 2026

Looking for the best expense manager app? Here is what SimpleMoney does, so you can judge for yourself. It is a free personal finance app for Android that keeps your money on your phone, reads your bank SMS for you, and works with no account and no internet.

What makes a good expense manager?

Most people stop using an expense tracker within weeks, because logging every payment by hand is tiring. A good one should remove that work, keep your data private and still be simple. SimpleMoney is built around those three things.

  • It adds expenses for you. Bank SMS alerts are read on your phone and turned into transactions. See the bank SMS expense tracker page.
  • It works offline, with no account. No sign-up, no email, no password. Everything is stored on your phone.
  • It covers more than daily spending. Budgets, loans and EMIs, money you lent or borrowed, subscriptions and recurring bills, in one app.
  • It has an optional AI assistant. Connect Claude or ChatGPT over MCP and ask questions about your spending. You approve every change. See AI expense tracker with MCP.

Everything in one expense manager and budget planner

  • Expense and income tracker: accounts, categories, transfers, and a clear monthly view.
  • Budget planner: set a limit for a category or a whole month and see what is left.
  • Loan and EMI tracker: progress, remaining balance and due dates, with reminders.
  • Debt tracker: who owes you and whom you owe, kept separate from daily spending.
  • Recurring payments and subscriptions: rent, premiums, SIPs and streaming, with a six-month forecast.
  • Multi-currency: each account in its own currency, with a combined total in your main one.
  • Statement import: PDF, CSV and Excel, including Google Pay, PhonePe and Paytm statements.
  • Backup you control: encrypted backup to your own Google Drive, and an app lock.

Free or paid?

Tracking is free. The free version has up to 7 budgets, a simple look and a few quiet ads. SimpleMoney Pro adds unlimited budgets, every look and accent colour, the AI assistant, and removes ads. Pro renews through Google Play and can be cancelled any time.

Frequently asked questions

Which is the best expense manager app for Android?

It depends on what you need. If you want an app that reads bank SMS, works fully offline, needs no account and also tracks loans and debts, SimpleMoney is built for exactly that. This page is written by the developer, so also compare it with other apps, see Cashew alternative and Money Lover alternative.

Does SimpleMoney need an account or internet?

No. There is no sign-up and it works offline. The internet is only used if you choose to back up to Google Drive or connect an AI assistant.

Is my financial data safe?

Your records stay on your phone. Bank SMS is read on the device. Backups go to your own Google Drive and can be encrypted with a PIN that is never stored. Read the privacy policy.

Is SimpleMoney free?

Yes, tracking your money is free. Pro is optional.
