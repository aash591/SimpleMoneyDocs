Common questions

What is SimpleMoney?

SimpleMoney is a free, private, offline-first expense manager and budget planner for Android. It reads bank SMS, and tracks loans, EMIs, debts, subscriptions and multiple currencies, with no account needed.

Does SimpleMoney read bank SMS automatically?

Yes. Bank and UPI SMS alerts are read on your phone and added as transactions. Nothing is uploaded and no bank login is needed.

Does SimpleMoney have AI?

Yes. An optional AI assistant connects your own Claude or ChatGPT to the app over MCP. It can answer questions about your spending and suggest changes, and you approve each one.

Is SimpleMoney free?

Yes. Tracking your money is free. SimpleMoney Pro adds unlimited budgets, every look, the AI assistant and no ads.

Does it work offline with no account?

Yes. There is no sign-up and your records stay on your phone.

No account needed. Your money stays on your phone. The free version has a few quiet ads, never while you add or browse; Pro removes them.
