09 Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about privacy, bank SMS, AI connectors, and how SimpleMoney works.

Does SimpleMoney ever upload my bank SMS or financial data?

Never. SimpleMoney runs entirely on your device. Bank and UPI SMS are parsed locally using on-device rules. We run no database servers that store your financial transactions, and none of your money data is ever uploaded, sold, or shared.

Can I use SimpleMoney completely without the internet?

Yes. All daily features — adding transactions, reading bank SMS, budgets, debts, loans, reports, multi-currency accounts, and statement import — work 100% offline. The internet is only accessed if you explicitly choose to back up to your personal Google Drive or connect an optional AI assistant.

How does the AI assistant work with Claude and ChatGPT?

SimpleMoney supports the open Model Context Protocol (MCP). When enabled, you can connect Claude or ChatGPT using a secure, time-limited 6-digit pairing code. You can ask questions in plain words like “what did I spend on dining last month?” or “teach the app this missed bank SMS”. The AI only suggests changes — nothing is written to your database until you personally review and approve it in the app.

How do I transfer my data if I switch to a new phone?

You can create an encrypted backup in More → Settings → Sync. Backups can be uploaded to your own Google Drive (hidden application folder) and encrypted with a custom PIN. When you install SimpleMoney on your new phone, restore with your PIN and your entire transaction history and accounts will be restored.

Is SimpleMoney free?

Yes. Core expense management, budgets, bank SMS reading, loans, debts, multi-currency accounts, and reports are completely free. SimpleMoney Pro is an optional one-time purchase that unlocks unlimited budgets, custom styling, and removes quiet ads.
