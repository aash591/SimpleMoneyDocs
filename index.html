One idea behind everything

Most apps mix up what the money was for with where it came from. SimpleMoney keeps them apart, so your reports stay honest.

Category = what it was for

Food, Fuel, Rent, Health. A category never belongs to an account.

Account = where it came from

Cash, HDFC Bank, a credit card, a wallet.

₹500 Food · HDFC Bank+₹200 Food · Cash=Food: ₹700 this month

A transfer from your bank to your wallet is its own thing — it moves money, it isn’t spending, so it never inflates your expenses.

Private by design

No account to create. No ads. No analytics or crash-reporting services. Your records live in a database on your phone, and the app has no server of its own. Backup to Google Drive is optional and goes to your Drive.
